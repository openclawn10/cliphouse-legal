ClipHouse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what ClipHouse collects and how we use it across our websites, our Whop community and course, and the ClipHouse Poster app.

1. Information we collect

From customers: name, email, and purchase details provided through Whop (we do not see full card numbers), and messages you send us. From TikTok, through the ClipHouse Poster app, only for TikTok accounts ClipHouse owns: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope, and the access tokens needed to upload and publish our own videos via the video.upload and video.publish scopes.

2. How we use it

To deliver courses and community access, support customers, and publish ClipHouse's own videos to ClipHouse's own TikTok accounts. We do not use TikTok data for advertising or profiling, and we do not access other people's TikTok accounts.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with service providers that run the Service (Whop for payments, Composio for secure storage of the TikTok connection, Google for storage), or when required by law.

4. Retention and security

TikTok tokens are stored encrypted by our integration provider and kept only while the connection is active. Disconnecting the app, or revoking it in TikTok settings, ends our access. Customer records are kept as long as needed for the purposes above and legal obligations.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing us. We respond within 30 days.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes.
